New · The notch island

The notch, put to work

On a MacBook with a notch, the black area around it becomes a live island. At rest it is two small ears; when something happens it opens just long enough to tell you — right where you are already looking.

Answer without switching apps. Permission requests stay at the notch until you choose, with optional hot keys: ⌃⌥Y to allow, ⌃⌥N to deny.

No notch? No problem. The island also lives as a pill on other screens, on every screen or only the main one, and steps aside for full-screen apps.

New · Layout fix

Typed in the wrong layout? It fixes itself.

  • Finish a word in the wrong layout and it is retyped in the right one, and the layout switches.
  • Double Shift converts the last word, or undoes a fix — and remembers the word.
  • ⌥⇧ converts the selected text.
  • English, Russian, Ukrainian, German, French, Portuguese and Spanish. No dictionaries to fill, nothing typed is stored, password fields and terminals are left alone.

Install

Two minutes, once

  1. Download AIHubBar-<version>.dmg from Releases.
  2. Open it and drag AI Hub Bar to Applications.
  3. Open the app. macOS says it can't check the developer — close the message.
  4. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

The app isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS asks once. After that it updates itself, and the first start opens an assistant that sets up your first profile.

Needs: macOS 14 or later (Apple Silicon or Intel). AI features need at least one AI service; the Mac tools work without any.

Privacy

Nothing in between

No server, no account, no analytics. AI Hub Bar talks only to the AI services you use; your files go only to the service you run a command with.

Keys stay in the Keychain. Never in logs or exported settings. Session hooks only write small status files on your Mac and come off in one click.

Nothing you type is kept. Layout fix looks at the word being typed and forgets it; clipboard history skips passwords and the apps you exclude.
