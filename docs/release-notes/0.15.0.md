## New

- **Find skills in plain words** — describe the task in your own words and press «Find by meaning with AI» under the Skills search: your model (Claude Code on your subscription, or the Anthropic API with your key) turns it into search words, the catalog and the open sources are searched on your Mac, and the model picks up to ten with a line on why each fits. It can only pick from what was found, and it never changes a verdict.
- **Skills for a project** — «Skills» in a project's card picks skills for that project. Its languages, tools and package names are read on your Mac and only these names go to your model, never a file's text; the picks are remembered until the project changes.
- **Open sources** — the globe in the Skills section lists every repository the well-known skill lists name (about 900), searchable without downloading anything. «Download and check» makes one a source; a search in your list also tells you how many more are in the open sources.
- **Descriptions in your language** — «Translate descriptions» above a list and on a skill's card translates them with your model; translations are remembered, the originals are a click away. Settings → Skills → «Help from your model» can translate them as soon as they appear.
- **Sign in to GitHub** (optional) — your own token, with no permissions, raises GitHub's limit from 60 to 5,000 requests an hour, so sources are checked without waiting. It stays in the Keychain and goes to api.github.com only.
- **AI economics** — a window with the API models side by side: prices, context, what a question, an agent task and a big task would cost with each, and what your last 30 days of Claude Code would cost at each model's prices. In the «More» menu of the panel and under the limits.
- **Stop All** — with two or more background tasks running, a red button in the header of «Agent sessions» stops them all on the second click.
- **What a background task used** — the journal of a background task shows its tokens and what the same work would cost through the API.
- **Sessions on other computers** (beta) — Settings → Agent sessions: add another Mac or a Linux server this Mac reaches over SSH with a key, and its Claude Code sessions show in the panel with the computer's name.
- **AI Hub Bar as an MCP server** — `AIHubBar --mcp` lets Claude Code and other agents read your limits, sessions and background tasks. Read-only; add it yourself with `claude mcp add`.

## Fixed

- **Skills checks** — a Slack, Discord or Telegram webhook with a placeholder for its secret (`xxx`, `<token>`, `YOUR_…`) in a guide no longer makes a skill «dangerous».
- **Safer sources** — names that differ only in letter case can't share a folder, one repository can't fill the disk (256 MB at most), and downloads never follow a redirect away from GitHub.
- **The notch's ears** go away over a full-screen video too.
