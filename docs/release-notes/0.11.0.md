## New

- **What's new** — after an update the panel shows once what changed.
- **Week pace** — the week limit shows whether you are ahead of or behind an even spend.
- **Antigravity quota** — Gemini and other models' quota with your plan in the limits. Turn it on in Settings → AI Services → Gemini, then open `agy` once.
- **Homebrew** — `brew tap olehdemishev/aihubbar https://github.com/OlehDemishev/AIHubBar`, `brew trust olehdemishev/aihubbar`, then `brew install --cask ai-hub-bar`.

## Since 0.10

- Settings are shorter: “Modules”, “Panel” and “Menu Bar” became one page, “What to Show”.
- Limits are a big number over a bar with the pace; rings are still there in the block's settings.
- The blocks that are not about AI fold into one “More” row at the bottom of the panel.
- The stop button of a background task stops on the second click.
- The Settings sidebar can no longer stay collapsed.
